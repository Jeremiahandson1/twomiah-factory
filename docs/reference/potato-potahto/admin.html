⭐ Reviews & Feedback

Adds a section on the homepage with a big "Leave a Google Review" button and a private feedback form below. Use the Google URL once your Google Business Profile is live; until then the button shows a friendly "coming soon" message.

When live, find your URL: go to your Google Business Profile → click "Get more reviews" → copy the short link, OR use the format https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID. Leave blank and the button will show a "coming soon" message instead.

Private feedback (the form below the Google button) is emailed through Netlify Forms. To set the recipient, log in to app.netlify.com → your site → Forms → Notifications → set the email above as recipient for the form named review-feedback.

🤝 Volunteer / Get-in-Touch Form

Adds a short signup form on the homepage where people can send their name, email, and a note. Use it for volunteer recruiting, general contact, anything you don't want to bury inside the catering form.

Submissions are emailed through Netlify's built-in form delivery. To change or add recipients, log in to app.netlify.com → your site → Forms → Notifications → set the email above as the recipient for the form named volunteer-signup. Once configured, every submission lands in this inbox.
