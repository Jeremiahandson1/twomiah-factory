# Restaurant (private events) 2 - Deposit and balance reminders, pay link, card on file

Feature ids: `event_payment_automation` (hidden, not ready; reminders, pay links, card on file) and `event_bank_payments` (hidden, not ready; paying and autopay from a US bank account, needs the first). Tests: `restaurant-event-depth.test.ts` ("#2 reminders, a pay link per instalment, a card on file"), `restaurant-event-sign-and-pay.test.ts` (6, deposit at signing) and `restaurant-event-bank.test.ts` (12, bank payments). Each test file is run on its own (`bun tests/runEach.ts`); run together in one process the food-service suites disturb each other's fake Stripe, as they already did before this change.

Leader: Perfect Venue scheduled payments / autopay - https://help.perfectvenue.com/knowledge/how-to-schedule-payments ; accept payments - https://help.perfectvenue.com/knowledge/how-to-accept-payments ; Tripleseat payments - https://tripleseat.com/features/

Not built: a retry of a returned autopay debit on a later day (the client is told and given the pay link; a closed or refusing account stops the autopay); refunds of a bank payment (Stripe refunds ACH payments in full and slowly; a refund is made in Stripe's dashboard and is not followed here); a notice to the venue's own inbox when a debit is returned (the client is emailed and the event shows it); `payment_method.automatically_updated`; the dispute Stripe can open on a debit long after it succeeded; a bank payments report. The bank option appears only when the venue's Stripe account has the ACH capability active (asked from Stripe on each page load).

Unverified: everything was proved against a fake Stripe (`tests/foodKit.ts`) that answers `us_bank_account` PaymentIntents, SetupIntents, mandates, the account's capabilities and the processing -> succeeded / requires_payment_method lifecycle as I understand Stripe documents it. I read Stripe's ACH Direct Debit page (docs.stripe.com/payments/ach-direct-debit: delayed notification up to four business days, verification by instant, automatic or micro-deposits, a mandate, failures at any time after initiation, the `us_bank_account_ach_payments` capability on connected accounts, full refunds only). I did NOT confirm from a readable Stripe page: the exact event names and order for every state (processing, requires_action), the failure-code names behind the plain-words reasons (`insufficient_funds`, `account_closed`, `no_account`, `invalid_account_number`, `debit_not_authorized`, `bank_account_restricted`; any other code is told in Stripe's own sentence), Stripe's test bank-account numbers, or the shape of `next_action.verify_with_microdeposits`. The `pm_bank_*` ids in the fake are the test's own. No live Stripe call has been made; nothing was seen in a real browser or on a real phone (Stripe's Payment Element has never been loaded for a bank account). The platform's Stripe Connect webhook is set to `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed`, `checkout.session.completed` and `charge.refunded` (apps/api, outside this change); for the quickest updates it should also deliver `payment_intent.processing`, `setup_intent.succeeded` and `setup_intent.setup_failed`. Without them the hourly pass (`runBankSettlement`) finds a settled or returned debit, and a verified bank, within the hour.

## Owner's check in Stripe test mode

Do this before switching these features on for a real venue. Use a test company with its Stripe account connected in test mode, ACH Direct Debit switched on in that Stripe account, a test client with your own email, and the features `event_payment_automation`, `event_proposals`, `event_contracts` and `event_bank_payments` on.

1. Make a $1,000 event with a $300 deposit and a $700 balance. Copy the deposit's pay link (event payments panel) and open it in a private window. Pay by card with Stripe's test card 4242 4242 4242 4242; confirm the deposit shows paid on the event, once.
2. On the balance's pay link tick "keep this card", pay by card, then make the next instalment due today and wait for the hourly pass; confirm one charge on the card and none more the same day. Save Stripe's declining test card (ending 0002) on another event and confirm the client is emailed with the pay link.
3. Send a proposal with two signers (the client, then the venue). Open the client's link and sign with a drawn signature, typed name and the consent tick; confirm the same page now offers the deposit and the event is not booked yet; sign as the second signer from the email it sends, and confirm the event books and the PDF shows both signatures.
4. On a new proposal, sign and pay the deposit on the same page by card and keep the card; confirm one payment and the card shown on the event.
5. On another pay link choose "Pay from my bank account" and use Stripe's test bank account for instant verification. Confirm the page says the payment is on its way, the invoice still shows the amount owed, the event's bank list says "Processing" and a second attempt is refused. When Stripe's test debit succeeds, confirm the page and invoice show it paid once, as a bank transfer, and the event books.
6. Repeat with Stripe's test bank account that fails the debit; confirm the client's email arrives once saying why, the invoice stays unpaid and the pay link works again.
7. Leave a bank account on file without paying (verify with Stripe's micro-deposit test amounts), make an instalment due today, and confirm the next hourly pass starts one debit that shows "Processing", the pass the next day starts none, and it is recorded only after Stripe settles it. Then remove the saved account from the pay page and confirm it is gone in Stripe.

## Row 18 (10/31): the fake checked against Stripe's documented shapes

Read against Stripe's API reference for every call these items make: PaymentIntent create with `confirm=true` and a saved `payment_method` (succeeded, or a 402 `card_declined`); SetupIntent create/retrieve with `usage=off_session` and `payment_method_types`; `payment_methods/:id` (a `card` with brand and last4; a `us_bank_account` with `bank_name`, `last4`, `account_holder_type`, `account_type`, `routing_number`), `/detach`; `mandates/:id` (`customer_acceptance.online.{ip_address,user_agent}`, `payment_method_details.us_bank_account.collection_method`); `accounts/:id` with `capabilities.us_bank_account_ach_payments`; a bank debit confirmed as `processing`, then `succeeded` or back to `requires_payment_method` with `last_payment_error`; Checkout Sessions with `line_items`, `payment_status`, `status`. All as documented. **One gap closed:** `next_action.verify_with_microdeposits` carries `microdeposit_type` (`amounts` or `descriptor_code`) as well as `arrival_date` and `hosted_verification_url`; the fake now answers it. Still only as documented, not as observed: the ACH failure codes in `last_payment_error.code` (`insufficient_funds`, `account_closed`, `no_account`, `debit_not_authorized`, `bank_account_restricted`, `invalid_account_number`), Stripe's test bank account numbers, and the order of the `payment_intent.processing` / `succeeded` / `payment_failed` events. The owner's check above is the proof.
