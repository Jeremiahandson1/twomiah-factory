## Recurring schedule patterns

| Capability | Leader(s) that have it | Source link | Our status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create recurring shifts, or many shifts at once, from several views. Specific patterns (weekly, every N weeks, monthly) are not spelled out | AlayaCare | [scheduling benefits](https://alayacare.com/?p=26569) | `matches`. Repeating shifts: every week, every other week, every 3 to 12 weeks, and monthly on the same weekday of the month (the 2nd Tuesday; a start on a 5th weekday means the last one). Monthly and every-3-or-more-weeks need the repeat feature; weekly and every other week never did. The leader's page names no patterns, so which of ours it also has is unknown. Edited shifts follow the same rules. **Several weekdays in one shift** (10/31, parity row 5, behind `care_shift_repeats`): `POST /api/schedules-enhanced` takes `daysOfWeek: [1, 3, 5]` and makes one row per weekday tied by a series id (`repeat_group_id`), all or none (a weekday already taken refuses the lot and names the day), and "every other week" counts the weeks from the same week for every weekday (Monday and Wednesday of the same weeks); a monthly or split shift stays one weekday at a time; the Schedule Hub sends the weekdays as one request and offers to stop all weekdays of a series together (`DELETE /api/schedules/:id?series=true`). Proof: `tests/homecare-depth-visits.test.ts`, `tests/homecare-parity-required-tasks.test.ts`; code `server/crm/homecare/core/repeats.ts`, `server/data/homecareCore.ts`, `server/crm/homecare/scheduling/schedulesEnhanced.ts` |

## Gaps and uncertainty

- Skip-with-reason on a required task: not found for AxisCare (the tutorial says nothing about skipping; the franchise guide only says complete or incomplete). For AlayaCare, a per-task comment exists, but one walkthrough does not state that completion is mandatory while two others say clock-out is blocked, so the documents disagree or differ by configuration.
- WellSky Personal Care: tasks can be managed in the mobile app per its listing, but required-task enforcement was not found.
- Recurring patterns (weekly, every N weeks, monthly): not found for AxisCare or WellSky. The AxisCare tutorial only mentions a calendar of next-week visits. AlayaCare confirms recurring shifts exist but not which cadences.
- AxisCare scheduling page and features overview do not mention recurrence.
- All AxisCare detail comes from a mobile tutorial PDF of unknown date and a 2021 franchise-hosted copy; newer behaviour may differ.

## Status summary
- Built before: required tasks (feature `care_required_tasks`) and monthly or every-3-to-12-weeks shifts (feature `care_shift_repeats`), both hidden until switched on. A required task blocks clock-out until done, or skipped or refused with a written reason. A clock-out the phone app saved while offline is recorded and flagged for the office, not blocked.

## Parity build, 10/31 — row 5: required tasks and repeats, finished
Status: **built**, behind `care_required_tasks`, `care_visit_signature` and `care_shift_repeats` (all hidden; not ready until the owner has tried them on a real phone).
- **Task days and time of day are read** (`core/taskDays.ts`): "Mon,Wed,Fri", "Tuesdays", "Mon-Fri", "weekdays", "weekends", "Daily" are understood; AM / PM tasks belong to a visit that starts before noon / is still going or starts at or after noon (a 10–2 visit has both). The visit's day and time are the agency's clock, never the server's. A Tuesday task holds only a Tuesday visit's clock-out, the checklist tells the phone which tasks belong to this visit (`appliesToVisit`), and days the office types that we cannot read are refused in plain words (when the feature is on) instead of silently meaning every day. Without the feature days and times stay labels, as before.
- **Client signature on every visit** — see the table row above. **EVV report that every assigned task was done or skipped with a reason** — see the table row above.
- **The phone greys Clock out** while a required task, a required in-visit item or the signature is open, and says what is left.
- **A repeating shift on several weekdays** — see the table row above.
- Tests: `tests/homecare-parity-required-tasks.test.ts` (28), `tests/mobile/required-tasks.test.ts` (13).
- **Not done / not seen:** not seen in a real browser or on a real phone (the signature pad, the greyed button, the series create in the Schedule Hub). The phone-call (IVR) clock-out still cannot mark tasks done or sign: it is never blocked and is flagged for the office (tasks, and now the missing signature) — completing tasks by phone call is row 16. A series of weekdays has no "edit the whole series" (changing the times or caregiver of every weekday at once) — only create and stop together; edits are per weekday. A monthly shift cannot take several weekdays in one request. The signature is not yet printed on the visit report PDF or the family portal. The Visit Documentation screen does not show the signature or the task report.
- Owner must check by hand: make one task required for one weekday only on a test client and try to clock out on another day; turn on "client must sign" and clock out on a real phone with and without a signature; create a Monday–Wednesday–Friday shift and look at the calendar.
