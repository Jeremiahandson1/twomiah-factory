## Observations
- None of the three leaders hides clinical words. AxisCare and WellSky both say "medication"; AlayaCare says "Medication" and "Vitals" outright. The softer, neutral labels are "Tasks", "Activities", "Care Plan", "Care Notes" and "Change in Condition".
- "ADLs" is the common daily-living term across AxisCare and WellSky (AlayaCare avoids it in the pages opened).

## Gaps and uncertainty
- AxisCare: a vitals section in the caregiver app: not found. A medication menu or reminder setup page: not found (only the FAQ sentence). help.axiscare.com was not reachable.
- AxisCare Care Vision question categories (whether ADLs, vitals or mood): not found; the page defers to sales.
- WellSky: medication reminders or vitals entry in the caregiver app: not found. The assessment PDF lists section names but not how they map to caregiver-app menus.
- AlayaCare: the "Welcome to Care Plan" article could not be fetched, so the "daily activities" wording rests on a single summary line and is unconfirmed.
- No leader page opened states a policy of avoiding clinical language for non-medical agencies; the observation above is inferred from the labels.

## Parity build, 10/31 — row 2: medication reminders that remind
Status: **built**, behind `care_medication_reminders` (hidden; not ready until the owner has tried it on a real phone).
- Each scheduled medicine carries **dose times** (`medications.dose_times`, 0053): set on the medicine form ("Dose times", agency clock), shown on the list. As-needed medicines have none. Start and end dates are respected.
- **Caregiver reminder.** A push (and an in-app notice) to the caregiver clocked in at that client, `leadMinutes` before the due time and for up to an hour after (so a late clock-in is still told). Not sent if the dose is already recorded, including a dose saved on the phone offline (matched on the tap time or the scheduled time). Once per dose per visit (`medication_dose_reminder`).
- **Office notice.** After a visit ends and `officeGraceMinutes` pass (default 60, so an offline phone has time to send), each dose that fell inside the visit with no record at all (given, refused, held and missed count as answers) is told to the office, once.
- **Agency settings** (company.settings.medicationReminders, `/api/medication-reminders/settings`, card on the medicine list): on/off, 0/5/10/15/30 minutes ahead, 15/30/60/120/240 minutes grace.
- **Caregiver's own settings.** Goes through the one alert door: the phone-notification switch applies. A dose reminder is urgent, so it goes through quiet hours unless the caregiver turned "still send urgent alerts" off. There is no separate medicine switch in the caregiver's settings (adding one would need a column on notification preferences; not done).
- Test: `tests/homecare-parity-medication-reminders.test.ts` (14 tests). Without the change it does not run (the module does not exist).
- **Not done / not seen:** not seen on a real phone or in a real browser. Tapping the push does not open the visit's medicine screen (the push carries the ids; the phone does not route on them yet). An office notice is not retracted if a save arrives after the grace. The weekday a dose applies (every other day, weekly) is not modelled: a time is due every day inside the start and end dates.

## Parity build, 10/31 — row 3: questions at clock-in and clock-out
Status: **built**, behind `care_visit_questions` (hidden; not ready until the owner has tried it on a real phone).
- The agency writes questions (`/crm/visit-questions`, "Clock-in questions" under Compliance): yes/no, one pick from a list, or a short written answer; asked at clock-in, clock-out or both; in the agency's order (up/down); can be switched off; may be **required**; may name the answer that **tells the office** (e.g. "no" to "Was the home safe?").
- The phone asks them on the clock-in screen ("Before you start") and in the clock-out sheet ("Before you go"). A **live** punch with a required question blank is refused with 409 `questions_required` naming each. A punch **saved on the phone without signal** is never refused: it is recorded and flagged as before, and whatever was answered is kept (the questions are kept on the phone from last time, so they are still asked at a door with no signal).
- Answers are kept on the visit (`visit_answer`), with the question's wording copied so the record does not change when the question is edited or deleted. An answer equal to the flag answer is marked on the record and told to the office once. The office reads them under "Answers" (last 30 days, "only answers that need a look").
- Tests: `tests/homecare-parity-visit-questions.test.ts` (10), `tests/mobile/visit-questions.test.ts` (8).
- **Not done / not seen:** not seen in a real browser or on a real phone. The answers are not yet printed on the visit report PDF or shown on the family portal. Questions are agency-wide (not per client or per service). No reorder by drag.
