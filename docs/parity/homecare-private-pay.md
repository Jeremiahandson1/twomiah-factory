Researched 2026-10-30 from public help and product pages only. All wording is paraphrased. Page contents were read through a summarising fetch step, so re-open a link before building to a detail.

## Gaps and uncertainty

- AxisCare Bill & Collect page does not mention retries or failed-payment notices; the full help centre (blocked) may. Not found for all three leaders.
- AxisCare autopay: the page only says staff can schedule it; whether it is per-invoice, recurring, or opt-in by family is not found.
- WellSky Personal Care: private-pay autopay, LTC billing, split billing and QuickBooks not found. WellSky's LTC revenue-cycle page covers long-term care facilities, not home care, so it was left out.
- AlayaCare: autopay, split billing (private-pay page silent) and QuickBooks not found. The split-by-cap example is from a vendor-sponsored article about one customer. The LTCI claim transmission method (fax, portal, clearinghouse) is not found.
- "Claim packet" as a named product feature: not found anywhere; the closest is AxisCare faxing claims with care notes. The packet contents above come from one insurer (MetLife); other carriers differ.
- AxisCare QuickBooks: whether customer records, credits or payments in the reverse direction sync is not stated; the page says data flows one way.
- Stripe retries and emails are documented for Billing subscriptions and invoices. For a bare off-session PaymentIntent we would need our own retry and notice logic (my reading, not stated on the Stripe pages). Stripe's exact error handling text for off-session declines was not retrievable (the page returned only an index).
- Stripe pages were fetched as markdown copies; dollar figures in the split example are rounded as written in the article.

## Status summary

- Built, hidden and marked not ready (`care_autopay`, `care_ltc_billing`): a family's card is saved on Stripe's own page, on the agency's own Stripe account, and charged for what is owed as each invoice is sent, with four tries on a decline, a plain-words email to the family and a notice to the office each time, a pause on cards that can never work, and an office screen to manage it; and a long-term care insurance payer type with a policy per client and a claim packet PDF per sent invoice. 27 tests are written (`tests/homecare-depth-privatepay.test.ts`: 21 autopay, 6 long-term care); I read them but did not run them.
- Never tested against the real thing: every Stripe call was tested against a fake. Whether Checkout setup mode gives a card that can be charged off-session, the real decline and error shapes, the idempotency behaviour and the consent text on Stripe's page all come from Stripe's docs and are unverified. Real email was not sent in any test.
- Left unbuilt, with what each needs: the QuickBooks push of home care invoices (a live QuickBooks sandbox company and a mapping decision); ACH bank payments (bank debit on the agency's Stripe account and authorisation wording); receipt emails after a successful charge (a template and a send); card-expiry warning emails (a daily job and a template); a family saving or changing their own card in the portal (a portal screen); sending the claim packet by fax or email (a provider account); real clock-in and clock-out timesheets and a logo in the packet; automatic reminders and write-offs.
- The owner must verify by hand: on the agency's own Stripe account, save a real card through Stripe's page, send a small invoice and watch it charge and land on the invoice once; then use Stripe's test cards for a plain decline, an expired card and a bank that needs the card owner, and read the emails the family receives. Confirm the Factory forwards `checkout.session.completed` (setup mode) and `payment_intent.*` events to the tenant in the connected-account case (the Factory lists those events, but I did not see them arrive). Have the consent wording looked at, and ask one real insurer whether the claim packet is enough.
- Known smaller faults for the owner: a failed-payment email goes to the billing contact stored on the client's private-pay payer first, then the portal email, then the client's email (follow-up 10/30; the only billing contact the data holds is the payer's, and an insurer's or referral source's billing office is never told); the retry scan runs about every 3.5 minutes, not hourly; both features stay hidden until the owner turns them on.
