# Home care parity: family and client notifications on caregiver clock in/out

Researched 2026-10-30. Headline: none of the three leaders' pages that were opened document an automatic family text or email when a caregiver clocks in or out. What they do document is a family portal plus account-level emails.

## Gaps and uncertainty
- Automatic text, email or app message to a family member or client when a caregiver clocks in or out: not found for AxisCare, AlayaCare or WellSky. AlayaCare's system notification list covers only employee-facing items and explicitly lacks client notifications; AlayaCare's late clock-in notification is described as legacy and not triggerable.
- Quiet hours: not found for any leader.
- Sender phone number for family texts (shared vs dedicated): not found for any leader.
- Family opt-in or consent capture wording: not found; WellSky and AlayaCare describe office-granted access only.
- help.axiscare.com was not reachable, so AxisCare may document more than its marketing pages show.
- The AlayaCare Family Portal overview article could not be fetched, and the notification channel list for the portal permission is unconfirmed (the Core and Advanced article says channels are not named).
- Third-party tools (for example SMS add-ons for WellSky) appeared in search results but were not opened and are not counted.

## Status summary
- Built: a text to a client's relatives when the caregiver clocks in and when they clock out, only for relatives the office marked as having said yes (and who have a phone number), from the agency's own texting service. One text per visit, relative and event, so a replayed or twice-sent punch cannot text twice. Tested in `tests/homecare-depth-family.test.ts` with a fake texting service, including through the real clock-in and clock-out routes.
- Rules that are fixed in code, not settings: no texts between 9 PM and 8 AM on the agency's clock (recorded as skipped, never sent later), and a punch that reaches us more than 30 minutes late (for example one saved on a phone with no signal) is skipped. A relative who is skipped is not told afterwards.
- Not built: email or app messages to families, a different quiet-hours window per agency, storing the wording the relative agreed to, a screen showing which texts were sent or skipped (the records exist in the database only), and editable text wording.
- The owner must check by hand: switch the feature on for a test agency, tick the yes for a relative with a real mobile number, clock a test caregiver in and out during the day and confirm both texts arrive from the agency's number; then reply STOP and confirm the next clock-in sends nothing to that phone. Also confirm the agency is happy to rely on a ticked box as the relative's consent.
