## Gaps and uncertainty
- help.axiscare.com was not reachable, so AxisCare detail comes from its public PDFs and marketing pages; its push/text delivery behaviour for open shifts is not documented there.
- AxisCare: how the office sees who was reached or who responded: not found. Bonus pay for a shift: not found.
- AlayaCare: send to all, or auto-target all qualified employees: not found (offers go to chosen employees; the finder only recommends). First-come rule among several acceptors: not found. Bonus pay: not found.

- No leader page opened documents shift-specific bonus pay.

## Status summary
- Built: the "Tell caregivers" button now really tells people. It sends an in-app notice and a phone push to every active caregiver who is free at that time (limited to the named caregivers when the posting was offered to some), and a text on request. The reply counts who was actually told, and a posting nobody could be told about is not marked as sent. Tested in `tests/homecare-depth-shifts.test.ts` with fake texting and push only.
- The count means "the in-app notice was saved". The push is attempted but its result is not counted, so "told 2" does not prove two phones buzzed.
- Left unbuilt: declining or hiding a shift, a days-ahead limit, an agency-wide visibility setting, whole-series offers, response deadlines, email, a sent/failed message record with resend, warnings before sending, and any paying of the pickup bonus. The two that need an owner decision are the pickup bonus (pay it or drop it) and whether to add decline.
- The "first to accept gets it" path and the first-tap race have no test.
- The owner must check by hand on a real phone: do a real "Tell caregivers" with a caregiver who has the app, confirm the phone buzzes and the notice shows on Notifications; send one text and then reply STOP from that phone and confirm the next text does not arrive.


## Parity build, 10/31 — row 18: pickup bonus, rewards and availability on the phone
Status: **built in part**, behind `care_pickup_rewards` (hidden; not ready until the owner has tried it in a real browser and on a real phone).
- **The pickup bonus is paid through the pay run.** Every fill of an open shift (first to accept, office approval, smart-fill, auto-fill) records the bonus in whole cents and who took it. Once the visit has been worked (a payable shift review for that caregiver, client and day) the bonus is added to the caregiver's line in the pay period holding the shift's date: shown apart ("Pickup bonus" on Payroll → Calculate and the pay stub), inside gross pay, kept on the approval (`payroll_records.bonus_pay`), and listed on its own line in the payroll CSV and the QuickBooks file. A posting the office reopens pays nothing. It is a flat amount: it does not change hours or the overtime split.
- **Office page** *Pickup bonus & rewards* (Scheduling): the pickups with worked / not worked yet, what earns points, who has how many, a hand adjustment, the reward list and the requests to hand over.
- **Phone:** *My hours & time off* and *Points & rewards* (More menu, shown only when the agency has the feature).
- Tests: `tests/homecare-parity-pickup-rewards.test.ts` (18), `tests/mobile/rewards-availability.test.ts` (24). The server test cannot run without the change (the module, routes, tables and columns do not exist).
- **NOT built / limits:** (1) **ADP, Paychex and Gusto hours files cannot carry a bonus** — they are refused (400, naming who and how much) when the dates include one, rather than leave it off; carrying it needs each provider's bonus earnings code, which is row 21. (2) A bonus is not folded into the overtime rate (the "blended regular rate" is row 19). (3) Points are not shown on the phone's open-shift cards, and a reward is not an automatic gift card — the office hands it over. (4) Time-off requests use the existing time-off records, and the phone shows only the last 15. (5) The office cannot yet see hours wanted next to a caregiver's name on the staffing screens, and matching does not yet score it (row 14). (6) Not seen in a real browser or on a real phone.
