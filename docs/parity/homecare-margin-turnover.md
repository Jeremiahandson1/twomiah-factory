## Gaps and uncertainty
- Formulas: no leader page opened defines margin, turnover rate or retention rate. We cannot say which fields (bill rate, pay rate, hours, mileage, hire and termination dates) they use. The only field-like hints are AlayaCare's margin inputs (mileage, overtime hours, per-visit) and its onboarding lag (hire to first shift).
- A true per-client profit/margin report: not found by that name for any leader; AxisCare has gross profit, AlayaCare has profitability tables, WellSky has a margin dashboard option.
- A dedicated turnover or retention report for WellSky: not found (TeamInsights page itself says turnover risk is future; one page says availability is unclear).
- AxisCare help centre articles on these reports were not reachable, so field lists may exist there.
- AlayaCare's data exploration page did not itself name margin, turnover or retention reports; those come from its blog posts.
- Industry figures in the leaders' blogs (turnover percentages, churn cost) are marketing statistics, not report outputs, and are left out.

## Status summary
- Built (feature `care_margin_reports`, hidden until switched on): a client margin report (billed minus caregiver pay, with overtime and mileage; with the pay-rules feature also state overtime, double time and paid travel) and a caregiver turnover report (hires, leavers, headcount, turnover, retention, 90-day retention). Tested in `tests/homecare-depth-margin.test.ts` and `tests/homecare-depth-payroll.test.ts` against a test database only.
- Not built: CSV or PDF download of either report, per-visit margin, comparing two periods, grouping by referral source or branch, applicant-to-hire ratio, hire-to-first-shift time, surveys, predictions, dashboards and a report builder. Each needs a decision from the owner on which are worth building; none needs an outside account.
- Known limits: "billed" is hours worked times the bill rate, not invoices sent. A caregiver who left is dated by their last visit because no leaving date is stored (adding a leaving date to the caregiver record would make this exact). One pay rate per caregiver is used for the whole window (the one in force on the last day).
- Out-of-date text: the screen footnote says travel time is not included and the screen has no travel or double-time column, although the report returns them when the pay-rules feature is on.
- Owner must check by hand: compare one client's margin and one month's turnover with your own spreadsheet using real data, and confirm the leaver dates look right. Nothing here has been run against a real agency's data.

## Parity build, 10/31 (branch cloud/homecare-parity) — rows 11, 12 and 13 of the brief "homecare.md"

### Row 11 — margin and turnover reports, finished

Status: **built** (not seen in a real browser). Behind `care_margin_reports` (still hidden). Every figure comes from one ledger of paid shifts (`core/margin.ts shiftLedger`), kept in integer cents, so every view adds up to the same total under the same filters. Test: `tests/homecare-parity-margin-turnover.test.ts` (11 tests; fails without the change). The item-10 suite `tests/homecare-depth-margin.test.ts` still passes (its "who left" row gained the new fields).

- CSV and PDF download of both reports (and of the per-visit report): `?format=csv|pdf` on `/api/margin-reports/client-margin`, `/visit-margin`, `/turnover`; the file helpers moved to `planning/reportFiles.ts`, shared with Reports & Analytics.
- Filters by payer, client, caregiver, branch and referral source on every margin view (`?payerId&clientId&caregiverId&branchId&referralSourceId`; `GET /filters` lists them). A client's payer is the payer of their newest active authorization; a branch is a service location (new `clients.branch_id`, `caregiver_profiles.branch_id`).
- Per-visit margin for a day or a month: `GET /visit-margin` — every paid visit with billed, pay, margin.
- Two periods side by side: `GET /compare?compareStart&compareEnd` — totals, change, per-client change.
- Trend chart (`GET /trend?by=week|month`, drawn with `web/.../lib/Chart.tsx`) and drill-down total → branch (the client's) → team (the caregiver's) → client (`GET /drill-down`).
- Hours invoiced (from invoice lines by service date) beside hours worked, per client and in the totals.
- Revenue by referral source, and the same grouping by payer, by branch and by department (**a department is the client's care type**) — department profit.
- A stored leaving date and reason on the caregiver's profile (`caregiver_profiles.left_on`, `left_reason`; Caregiver › Edit › "Leaving"), so turnover is exact; deactivating a caregiver dates it today on the agency's clock, reactivating clears it; the report says which departures still fall back to the last visit. Branch, team, department and manager on the profile too.
- Applicant-to-hire ratio (applications in the window; hired = status hired or a hired user), hire-to-first-shift (hire date → first clocked visit), call-outs (absences + visits cancelled as "caregiver called out") and missed visits (no-show alerts) per caregiver, cost per hire (expenses filed under hiring / recruiting / recruitment / onboarding / background checks / job ads ÷ hires), payroll accuracy (shifts reviewed vs corrected by the office, pay periods disputed vs confirmed) — all on `GET /turnover`.
- Known limits: mileage has no caregiver team, so in the drill-down it sits under its branch as "Mileage (no team)"; the trend dates mileage to the window's last bucket. "Billed" is still hours × the client's rate (an invoice is the hours-invoiced column).

### Row 12 — report builder, dashboards, scheduled reports

Status: **built** (not seen in a real browser). Behind `care_report_builder` (hidden). Office page Reports & dashboards (`/crm/report-builder`); API `/api/report-builder` (`planning/reportBuilder.ts`; engine `core/reportBuilder.ts`, `core/reportSchedules.ts`; files `planning/reportRenderers.ts`). Test: `tests/homecare-parity-report-builder.test.ts` (15 tests; fails without the change).

- Financial, authorization-utilisation and caregiver dashboards (`GET /dashboards/financial|authorizations|caregivers`): margin totals and by branch / referral source / payer / department, the weekly margin trend, invoiced / collected / payroll by month, receivables by age; each active authorization's units used against the pace of its window ("running out early" / "under-used" / "on pace"); headcount, 90-day turnover, on-time share, underused staff, missed visits, cancellations by week, and the retention tile when row 13 is on.
- A custom report builder over the visit fields (`FIELDS`: 35 fields — client, caregiver, branch, team, care type, referral source, payer, schedule, clock, lateness, approval, payable minutes and payroll status, invoiced amount and hours, signature and why not, tasks done / skipped, forms done / missed, ADLs, the client's rating, the note); filters by rule per field type; sort; group with sums; run on screen or download CSV / PDF; saved, copied (`POST /reports/:id/copy`), and copied from a **standard library** of nine (`LIBRARY`) then edited. Pay fields are shown only to someone who may read payroll.
- Custom dashboards (`dashboard_layout`): up to 12 tiles from a catalogue of 14 (`TILES`), **profit margin by location (branch) and by referral source** among them; a tile the reader may not see is withheld, never an error.
- Scheduled report emails (`report_schedule`): any of the seven kinds (client margin, margin per visit, turnover, daily manager, caregiver performance, workforce, one of your saved reports) as CSV or PDF, daily / weekly on a weekday / monthly on a day, at an hour of the **agency's clock**, over the last N days, to named staff. **Encrypted**: the file is stored AES-256-GCM at rest (`report_delivery`, the same key that seals SSNs) and the mail carries only a link that opens after sign-in for a named recipient (or a manager) for 14 days; the scan (visit jobs, every 5 minutes) claims each due day once, so two instances never send twice; a missed whole day is not sent out of the blue; "Send now" by hand.
- A daily manager report (`GET /daily-manager?date=`): scheduled / completed / in progress / late / missed / cancelled / never clocked, hours, open shifts unfilled, incidents, new applicants, open family requests, punches awaiting approval, authorizations and credentials ending in 14 days, and the day's billed / pay / margin; downloadable and schedulable.
- Workforce analytics (`GET /workforce`): hours per caregiver against the hours they want and the most they will do, utilisation, **underused** (a quarter or more under what they asked for) and overloaded, call-outs and missed visits, **cancellation trend by week** by caregiver / client / reason.
- Caregiver performance report (`GET /performance`): per caregiver, visits scheduled / completed / missed / cancelled by call-out, late clock-ins and late clock-outs (past the grace), on-time share, ADL (task) completion, form completion, signed share, client ratings, hours.
- Not done: Excel (.xlsx) as a third download format — CSV opens in Excel; AxisCare's "80+ fields" count is not matched one for one (ours are the fields a visit carries today).

### Row 13 — caregiver satisfaction and retention risk

Status: **built** (not seen in a real browser or on a real phone). Behind `care_caregiver_surveys` (hidden). Office page Caregiver satisfaction & retention (`/crm/caregiver-retention`); phone More › "How is it going?" (`apps/mobile/app/(care)/survey.tsx`); API `/api/caregiver-surveys` (`hr/caregiverSurveys.ts`; engine `core/caregiverSurveys.ts`, `core/retentionRisk.ts`). Tests: `tests/homecare-parity-retention.test.ts` (9 tests) and `tests/mobile/caregiver-survey.test.ts` (12 checks); both fail without the change.

- Surveys with the agency's own questions (1–5, yes/no, free text; a standard six to start from), asked at set points: after the first visit with a client, N days after hire (90 by default), every N days; each point asked once (`caregiver_survey_request`'s unique key); the scan runs with the visit jobs and catches up 14 days, never older. The caregiver gets a push through the same door as every caregiver alert (their notification settings apply; the push opens the survey screen) and an in-app notice; the office can also ask by hand.
- A score under the survey's alert line (default 3 of 5) raises an office notice at once with the caregiver's words.
- Results by branch, by manager and by caregiver, a monthly trend, the response rate, every answer, and who has not answered.
- A retention dashboard: the satisfaction trend, satisfaction by branch and manager, the number at risk, and the risk trend.
- An at-risk score per caregiver (0–100; low / watch / at risk) with its trend (a daily snapshot, `caregiver_risk_snapshot`) built from our own signals, **every reason shown in words**: hours dropped (last 4 weeks vs the 8 before), call-outs, missed visits, a low or middling survey score, surveys left unanswered, the first 90 days (and under six months), gone quiet (no visit in 14 / 30 days), under half the hours they asked for.
- Not done: nothing a leader describes is left out; the leaders' own weightings are unpublished, so the points per signal are ours (`scoreRisk`) and the owner may want to tune them after a month of real data. Survey by email or text is not offered — the phone app is the channel (AxisCare's is app push too).
