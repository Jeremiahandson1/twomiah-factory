## Gaps and uncertainty
- Formulas: no leader page opened defines margin, turnover rate or retention rate. We cannot say which fields (bill rate, pay rate, hours, mileage, hire and termination dates) they use. The only field-like hints are AlayaCare's margin inputs (mileage, overtime hours, per-visit) and its onboarding lag (hire to first shift).
- A true per-client profit/margin report: not found by that name for any leader; AxisCare has gross profit, AlayaCare has profitability tables, WellSky has a margin dashboard option.
- A dedicated turnover or retention report for WellSky: not found (TeamInsights page itself says turnover risk is future; one page says availability is unclear).
- AxisCare help centre articles on these reports were not reachable, so field lists may exist there.
- AlayaCare's data exploration page did not itself name margin, turnover or retention reports; those come from its blog posts.
- Industry figures in the leaders' blogs (turnover percentages, churn cost) are marketing statistics, not report outputs, and are left out.
