## Commissions

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Commission on the total sale amount, the signed contract — AccuLynx. On planned revenue — JobNimbus | A plan "% of the contract (before tax)" pays on everything invoiced on the job before sales tax, worked out once, when the job is Collected (`server/crm/roof/commissions.ts`). It is on what was actually billed, not a planned figure | **Matches** |
| 2 | Commission on gross profit (profit after material and labour are paid) — AccuLynx. On planned profit — JobNimbus | A plan "% of gross profit" pays on the contract less what the job cost: material orders placed, the crew's pay, hours clocked, expenses and vendor bills — the same figures the Job Costing screen shows. Before this change a margin plan could never pay by itself because no cost basis was known | **Matches** (actual profit at Collected; JobNimbus's is planned) |
| 3 | Flat fee — JobNimbus; a fixed dollar amount per job (pre-commission) — AccuLynx | "Flat amount per job" plan, paid when the job is Collected | **Matches** |
| 4 | A rate per salesperson — AccuLynx. Default commissions assigned to the job's salesperson — JobNimbus | A plan can name the people it pays; it pays the job's **sales rep** (`assignedSalesRepId`), not the crew's slot or whoever typed the lead in. A plan naming nobody pays any rep who has no plan of their own | **Matches** |
| 5 | Different rates by job-profit threshold — AccuLynx | A plan names what it is **measured on** — the contract, or the job's gross profit (`commission_plan.measured_on`). A percentage and the brackets of a tiered plan are read against that figure: "5% under $5,000 of profit, 8% from there" is a plan. The whole figure falls in one bracket (not split), the way the shared tiers have always worked. Roofing's plan form offers Tiered and the choice (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 6 | A company minimum profit, in percent or dollars — AccuLynx | The company's money rules (`roof_money_setting`, `/api/roof-money/settings`, commissions:update — owner and admin) hold a floor: a share of the contract the job must have kept as gross profit, or dollars of gross profit. A job under it pays **no plan** (profit or contract, flat or tiered) until its figures clear it; it is looked at again each time the job is followed up (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 7 | More than one commission on a job — JobNimbus; additional (office, estimator, canvasser) and management (production, sales manager) commissions on gross profit, net profit or total sales — AccuLynx | A job carries its sales rep and the other people named on it (`job_person`: estimator, canvasser, office, production manager, sales manager — several per role, set on the job page or `PUT /api/job-people/:jobId`, jobs:dispatch). A plan pays **one role** (`autoEarn` `job_estimator` …) and every person holding it whom the plan pays (a plan that names people pays only them), automatically when the job is paid, on **total sales** (the contract), **gross profit** or **net profit** — the gross profit less what the job's other plans paid out, worked out last, so no plan reads a profit it has itself reduced. Someone added to a job already paid is paid at once; someone who has left the team is not (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 8 | A commission is ready to request on a job status (Approved, Completed or Invoiced), optionally only with a $0 balance — AccuLynx | A plan names when it is ready (`commission_plan.trigger_on`): **Approved** (job reaches Signed), **Completed** (final inspection), **Invoiced**, or **Collected** — the default, every invoice that went out paid in full, as before. An early trigger can add **only when nothing is owing** (`zero_balance_only`: every invoice that went out is paid). It is made once per plan and person (`commission.earn_key`, a unique index). A plan on profit waits for Completed at the earliest (the costs are not in before then) and so does any plan while a company minimum profit is set; before the job is paid the contract is what was billed so far or the signed contract, whichever is more. An early trigger applies to jobs started after the plan was switched on — a job already past the stage waits to be paid, so switching a plan on never pays for the past. Run from the stage change (Advance, the job form's status) and from each payment (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 9 | Pre-commissions: a draw when the job is turned in — AccuLynx | A draw (`POST /api/commission-draws`, commissions:create; the Commissions screen's "Give a draw") is a commission line (source `draw`) for a person who holds the plan's role on a job that has reached Approved. When the plan pays, the commission is made for **what is left**: the draw and the commission add up to the plan's figure, never more; a draw as large as the commission leaves nothing more to pay; a voided draw comes off nothing. No draw once the plan has paid. It is paid, voided and statemented like any commission (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 10 | Earned, approved and paid states; payouts with a date — AccuLynx, JobNimbus | pending → approved → paid, disputed, void and reassign, a second person approves (nobody below an admin signs off their own pay), an audit trail, statements | **Matches** (existing) |
| 11 | A rep sees what they earn on each job and their earnings by year — AccuLynx | The rep sees their own commissions and a statement over any dates; never anyone else's. **Checked 10/31: the screen did not print the job on a line** (the Earnings table had no job column and the statement none). Fixed: the list and the statement now carry the job's number (`jobNumber`, joined on the server), and the Earnings table, the on-screen statement and the printed statement each have a Job column (a dash where a commission was recorded against no job) (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 12 | Which roles see commissions, set per company — AccuLynx | The owner and admin always see everyone's commissions and how plans are worked out; the company chooses which of the manager, field and read-only seats do too (`roof_money_setting.commission_viewer_roles`, the Commissions screen's Rules tab, changed by a seat that may change plans). The default is the manager, as it always was. Done through the shared permissions (`companyGrants`: a role listed is granted commissions:read, a choosable role left out is refused it), so every route, the sign-in payload and View As agree; the routes follow within seconds, an open screen at the next sign-in. Everyone always sees their own earnings (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 13 | A commission disclaimer for people who collect commissions — AccuLynx | The company's words (`roof_money_setting.commission_disclaimer`, the Rules tab, `PUT /api/roof-money/settings`) are shown to a person before they see their own commissions, and they press I accept (`POST /api/commission-disclaimer/accept`). The words they accepted, the version and the time are kept (`commission_disclaimer_acceptance`; a later edit never rewrites it). Until they accept, their OWN lines are withheld from the list (flagged) and their statement is refused; other people's are unaffected. Changing the words raises the version and everyone accepts again; the Rules tab lists who has accepted among the people who have a commission (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |
| 14 | A full refund takes the commission back | A Collected commission hangs on the job's last-paid invoice; refunding that invoice in full cancels it (or, if already paid out, adds a negative line). **Decision 10/31 for a refund in full of an EARLIER invoice of the job:** the job is no longer paid in full, so the job's Collected commissions are taken back the same way (cancelled if not yet paid out, a negative line of the same size if paid) — all of it, not a share, because a share cannot be worked out fairly on a profit or a bracket; the office records the right amount by hand after the refund, and the job is not re-earned on its own (the cancelled commission still stands for it). A PARTIAL refund changes nothing. A commission made at an earlier trigger (Approved, Completed, Invoiced) is not taken back — that plan was written to pay before the money is in — and a draw already paid out stays. The leaders' pages say nothing, so this is our rule (`roof/commissions.ts` takeBackForRefund; `tests/roof-commissions-refund.test.ts`) | **Different by design** (decided 10/31) |

Nothing to import | **Matches** |
| 16 | Labour: Work Orders imported — JobNimbus; expenses on the financial worksheet — AccuLynx | The crew assigned to the job is paid *pay per square × the job's squares* (shown only to a seat with margin:read); hours clocked on the job are costed as before, and then the crew's pay is not counted beside them. **A work order can carry a lump-sum price for the whole job** (`work_order.price`, migration 0058; set on the work-order form by a seat with margin:read, which is also the only seat that sees it; never on the crew's own link). While the order is sent, accepted or completed, the sum of the job's priced orders is the crew labour, **in place of** the pay by the square; a draft, rejected or cancelled order is not spend; hours clocked on the job still win (the same labour is never counted twice). A crew with no rate and no priced order, or a job with no squares, shows "not known" instead of $0 (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) |

| 19 | Planned against actual — JobNimbus; change against the original signed contract — AccuLynx | Estimated against actual with a variance on cost, labour, material and hours (existing screen), now with the roof's figures in both | **Matches** |
| 20 | Commission payouts change the job's profit — JobNimbus (paid payouts affect Actual Profit) | The commissions a job's people have **earned** on it (any live commission — pending, approved, paid or disputed; void and cancelled ones are not, and a taken-back line nets off; a draw counts as the part of the commission paid early) are a cost of the job: the Job Costing screen, its roll-up and its profit-by-month are after them (`actual.commissionCost`, in `totalCost`; a Commissions figure on the Roof card, and who earned what for a seat that reads commissions). **No circle:** the commission engine asks job costing for the costs *before* commissions, so a plan on gross profit — or the company minimum profit — is worked out on the profit before any payout, however often the job is followed up and whoever is added late; a net-profit plan still reads the profit less what the *other* plans paid. The variance against the estimate, a project budget's cost to date and the work-in-progress schedule are read before commissions (the estimate has none, and commissions are not cost of completion). We count earned, not only paid (JobNimbus counts paid), so the profit does not jump when a commission is marked paid (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31; earned rather than paid, by choice) |
| 21 | A job-profitability report across jobs — AccuLynx | The roll-up of every job's invoiced, cost, profit and margin, by month, filterable by stage and date | **Matches** |
| 22 | Job profit fed from QuickBooks or Sage Intacct — AccuLynx | Reads this CRM's own ledgers; QuickBooks is a separate sync | **Different by design** |
| 23 | Worksheet approval before it counts — AccuLynx; access levels No access / View / Create & Edit / Delete — JobNimbus | Access: Job Costing needs reports:read (owner, admin, manager); a crew's pay and a work order's price need margin:read; who earned what on a job needs commissions:read. **Approval, built 10/31, off by default:** a company switches on "Require an approved worksheet" in its money rules (`roof_money_setting.require_worksheet_approval`, the Commissions screen's Rules tab). A job's worksheet is then waiting until an owner or admin (reports:update) approves it from the Job Costing screen (`POST /api/job-worksheet/:jobId/approve`; the figures seen — invoiced, cost, profit before commissions — are kept with who and when, and a job note is written; `reopen` takes it back). Until it is approved: a commission on profit, and every commission while a minimum profit is set, is not made (it is made the moment the worksheet is approved); the job is listed marked "Not approved" and its profit is left out of the Job Costing totals and the profit by month. The job's own costing screen still shows its figures to the approver. **Not covered:** the work-in-progress schedule (reports) and the project budget screen still read every job's cost, as cost to date is not profit (10/31) | **Matches** (10/31) for access and approval |

## What this change builds
- Commission Tracking and Job Costing are offered to roofing (`packages/tenant-backend/src/featureRegistry.ts`); both are existing ids, so nothing is newly registered. A business sees the menu rows only with the feature on and the permission (the shared menu's own gates: commissions:read, reports:read).
- A plan can pay the job's sales rep when the whole job is paid (`job_sales_rep`): `server/crm/roof/commissions.ts`, run after money lands on an invoice (`server/crm/roof/index.ts`) and when the office moves a job to Collected by hand (`server/crm/roof/jobs.ts`). Behind a switch only roofing turns on (`salesRepAutoEarn` in `packages/tenant-backend/src/commissions/commissions.ts`; the other trades are refused the choice).
- Job Costing reads a roof job's material orders, its crew's pay and its supplements (`server/crm/roof/jobCosts.ts`, `server/crm/field/jobCostingService.ts`); the screen shows them (`packages/tenant-ui` JobCostingPage).
- Crew: pay per square (migration `0046_roof_crew_pay`, `crew.pay_per_square`), withheld without margin:read.
