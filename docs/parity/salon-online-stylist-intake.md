## Decisions and limits to know

- **Off by default.** `client_stylist_pick` and `intake_forms` are registered not-ready (hidden). With neither on, online booking is exactly as before: no stylist list, no forms. A stylist choice sent to a salon with the switch off is refused (400).
- Turning the choice on is the owner's (company:update); so is building forms. Anyone who reads clients can read forms and answers.
- Forms are shown on the booking's page after the booking exists. Unless the form is marked “before an online booking is confirmed”, a client who skips it still has a booking. A booking made at the desk or by staff is never held for forms (the client is with them).
- A held-for-forms booking uses the salon's existing hold length (the booking settings' deposit hold); it has no separate setting. Forms come before the deposit or card step; that step gets its own hold from the moment the forms are done (`booking.hold_from`).
- Drawing a signature in the web page needs JavaScript; without it the form is refused with a sentence saying to sign (nothing is skipped). The phone sends the pad's strokes as a fixed-shape SVG; the server only accepts that exact shape and draws it to a picture.
- A form is for “every booking”, a service, a membership plan, or both: a client is asked if any of these match. A form limited to services shows on a client's chart only after they have answered it (the chart does not know which service they will book).
- A “Send to clients” link and its answer do not need online booking switched on; they need `intake_forms`.
- The web chart can send a form to one client; filling a form in *with* the client is on the phone (and the API), not on the web chart.
- Not seen in a real browser or on a real phone: the pages, the signature box, the settings screen and the phone screen were checked through the real server and by their rules, not by eye.
- Nothing here takes a card or a payment.
- Proving tests: `apps/unified/tests/salon-stylist-intake.test.ts` — fails without the change (no stylists listed, `who` ignored, no forms routes); `apps/unified/tests/salon-intake-depth.test.ts` (13 tests — membership forms, expiry, photos and files, drawn signature, hold before confirm, forms then card, sending, the phone's fill, deleting a client) and `tests/mobile/intake-form.test.ts` (the phone's rules and chart section).
